<h2 id="objective">🎯 Objective</h2>

The project brings together the comfort of a neighborhood cafe, storybook woodland animals, and everyday generosity of paying for someone else's meal. Its gentle pace and hand-painted setting make a small act of kindness the heart of the experience. The imagination of the Oakenburrow community became a reality with Lovable, Claude, and ChatGPT.

<h2 id="story">🦌 Story</h2>

<p>
  Oakenburrow Cafe invites you to slow down inside a welcoming cafe
  tucked into the hollow of an old oak. Play as Fern, a quiet deer,
  settle in for a visit, and share a little kindness with the woodland
  community. Choose something from the seasonal menu, enjoy it at your
  own pace, and leave a carved meal token so another neighbor can eat
  too. 
</p>

<hr>

<h2 id="how-to-play">🎮 How to Play</h2>

<ol>
  <li>Step inside the hollow oak and take a seat.</li>
  <li>Browse the cafe menu and choose food or a drink.</li>
  <li>Meet the woodland regulars and enjoy your meal.</li>
  <li>Settle your bill and leave a carved token for a neighbor.</li>
  <li>Fern has a $100 card budget for the visit.</li>
</ol>
